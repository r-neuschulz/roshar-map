# Sinovar

> .Te uorld is Canging outsahyd but Te Sin seem determahyned too remain Te seym

.**Sinovar** is a naSuhn on Te uestern mainland of roSar .due too Te #[mountains](locations/misted-mountains) separeyting it frohm Te rest of Te kontinent Sinovar is distinkt boT ekologikallee and kulturallee frohm Te rest of roSar .Sinovar nativez ahr knoun as Te **Sin**

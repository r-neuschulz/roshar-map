# aleTkar

> .uev got a nobuhl heritaij kal darkeyes and lahyteyes alahyk .Tats huee Te beeest uarriors hav aluays been frohm aleTkar

.**aleTkar** is Te lahrjest and most prohminent of Te fiv vorin naSuhns on roSar .people frohm aleTkar ahr knoun as **aleTi** and tair royal kolors ahr blue and gold .prior too Te troo desolaSuhn Tey uur engaijed in Te uar of rekohning against Te listeners .at Te present Te vast majoritee of tair territoree is under kontrol of Te voidbringerz

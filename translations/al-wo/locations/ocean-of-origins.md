# oSuhn of aurigins

.Te **oSuhn of aurigins** is a bodee of ueyter east of roSar touards Te aurigin

.Te Sohrs of Te oSuhn of aurigins ahr sparslee populeyted uiT Te siteez of #[neu natanan](locations/new-natanan) and daunz Sadau being Te onlee notuhbuhl settlements .Tis is lahyklee bekauz hahystorms meyk landfaul Tair and as suC hit Tat koast uiT Te greeytest fohrs .too Te east Te oSuhn of aurigins presumuhbuhle konnekts too Te #[endles oSuhn](locations/endless-ocean) on Te ohposahyt sahyd of Te planet huahyl frohm norT and souT itz braketed bahy #[steamueyter oSuhn](locations/steamwaterocean) and #[souTern depTs](locations/southern-depths)

.Te aurigin of storms huair Te hahystorms aurigineyt lies at Te far end of Te oSuhn of aurigins .Tair ahr multiple legends talking uhbout Tair being an inhabahyted island Tair populeyted bahy people kauled uvara in suhm verzhuhns of Te storee .Te verzhuhn told in neu natanan says Tat Tey ahr sailors hoo uur lost huahyl travelling Te oSuhn of aurigins and Tat uuhn daee Tey uill kuhm bak too reklaim natanatan

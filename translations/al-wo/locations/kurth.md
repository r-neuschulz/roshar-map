# kurT

.**kurT** also knoun as Te **sitee of lahytning** is a sitee in norTeastern #[rira](locations/rira) .it is situeyted on an island in Te #[reSi sea](locations/reshi-sea) just off Te east koast of Te main riran landmas

.kurT uas uuhns Te kapital of Te silver kingdom of riSir and an oaTgeyt uas konstrukted Tair too serve Te naSuhn and itz people .after Te faul of riSir Te sitee beseym a part of Te naSuhn of rira .in Te kourse of her researC into Te oaTgeytez #[jasnah kholin](characters/jasnah) identified kurT as Te lahyklee sahyt of an oaTgeyt .huahyl planning hau too unahyt Te naSuhns of roSar in Te ueyk of Te everstorm members of #[dalinar kholinz](characters/dalinar) koaliSuhn hohped too ohpen and sekure Te kurT oaTgeyt .during Te troo desolaSuhn Te fohrseez of #[odium](characters/odium) sekured Te sitee and held itz oaTgeyt bahy alahying Temselvez uiT Te rirans and #[iriali](locations/iri)

.kurT is uell knoun as far auaee as #[aleTkar](locations/alethkar) .in his youT #[kaladin](characters/kaladin) had heard stories uhbout kurT #[raul elorim](locations/rall-elorim) and oTer farauaee lokales Tat enkouraijed his desire too join torol sadeasez armee in aurder too see huat oTer lands ahr lahyk

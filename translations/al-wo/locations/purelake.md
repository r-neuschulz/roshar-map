# Te pureleyk

> ...Te pureleyk seemed too eXtend fohrver uahyd as an oSuhn .rok outkrohpz brohk Te leyk near Te koast but inuard Te pureleyk greu placid mohr emptee

.Te **pureleyk** is a Sallau inland leyk lokahyted in norT sentral roSar .Te people of Te region ahr knoun as **pureleykerz** and ahr eTnikallee selaee

.Te pureleyk is influenst in suhm uaee bahy #[kultivaSuhn](characters/cultivation) .it is unknoun if Se has a sekondaree perpendikularitee in Te pureleyk

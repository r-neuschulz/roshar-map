# marabeTia

> .Te marabeTians hav a saying fohr Tohz hoo refyooz too see Te truT of a situaSuhn .you hav eyes of red and blue Tey saee .red fohr blood driping .blue fohr Te ueyter .it is said Tat Teez too Tingz ahr aul Te kriminals see .usuallee Tey ahr attaked uiTin uuhn daee .and yet most still uiS too teyk Tat Cahns Tey prefer Te fals hohp

.**marabeTia** is a naSuhn on roSar uhkupahying Te isTmus separeyting Te #[pureleyk](locations/purelake) frohm Te #[reSi sea](locations/reshi-sea) .it is in Te norT in selaee lands besahyd Te sea

.Te marabeTian people ahr knoun too hav a great fondnes fohr debeyt Te sitee has manee pedestals at intersekSuhns huair men kan stand and proklaim his arguments .Te residents ahr knoun too karree around overripe fruit fohr diskouraijing Tohz Tey disagree uiT

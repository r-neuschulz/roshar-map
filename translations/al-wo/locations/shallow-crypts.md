# Sallau kripts

> .ahy uouldnt send mahy oun broTer aSohr Tair uiTout guards and hes killed seventeen men uiT his bair hands he has

.Te **Sallau kripts** is a port settlement on Te souTern ej of Te #[frostlands](locations/frostlands)

.Te Sallau kripts is konsahyderd too bee a veree dangerous sitee bahy sailors hoo knau it uell .Te sitee itself is unremarkuhbuhl in uhpeeruhns .it is surrounded bahy several similar sister siteez in Te seym region .due too Te lokaSuhn of Te Sallau kripts Tair is a kommonlee traveled korridor running Trou Te sparslee populeyted frostlands Tat konnekts Te sitee uiT Te #[Sattered plains](locations/shattered-plains) and Te aleTi uarkamps .even in Tis korridor it kan bee eXpensiv too run a karavan too Te plains uiTout suffisient uairez .Te people of Te Sallau kripts often treyd sleyvez too karavans of merCants hoo transport Tem too Te Sattered plains fohr sale .Te sitee also does suhm treyd bahy sea manaijed bahy Te harbormaster

# tu bayla

.**tu bayla** is a naSuhn on roSar

#[ru parat](locations/ru-parat) .and #[fu namir](locations/fu-namir) ahr siteez in Te region .it borders #[jah keeved](locations/jah-keved) too Te east Te #[reSi sea](locations/reshi-sea) too Te norT a mountain ranj too Te souT and Sairez land borders uiT #[yulaee](locations/yulay) and #[marabeTia](locations/marabethia) too Te uest as uell as fohrming Te eastern koast of Te #[pureleyk](locations/purelake) .it seems too hav at least uuhn river Tat runs into Te pureleyk and anoTer Tat runs into Te reSi sea and maee also klaim suhm of Te reSi iahylez klohz too itz koast .it is fohrmed frohm parts of Te silver kingdoms of sela tales and valhav

.itz people ahr nomadik .tyn greu up Tair #[uriTiru](locations/urithiru) .lays in Te mountain ranjez too Te souT of tu bayla

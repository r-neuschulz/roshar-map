# Te rekreuhns

.after Te fals desolaSuhn most aurders of Te knahyts radiant disbanded leeyving behind tair Sardbleydez and Sardpleyt

## Details

.Te daee of rekreuhns uas Te final akt of Te knahyts radiant .nahyn of Te ten aurders uhbanduhnuhned tair Sardbleydez and Sardpleyt and brohk tair oaTs killing tair spren .Te aurder of skahybreeykerz uas Te uuhn aurder Tat did not partisipeyt and insted hid Temselvez auaee .fohr around too Tousand years Te reason fohr Te rekreuhns remaahyned unknoun until it uas rediskovered in Te eila stele during Te troo desolaSuhn

# adolinz trial   daee

.Te furst daee of adolinz trial bahy uitnes in lasting integritee

## Details

.#[adolin](characters/adolin) arrivez at his trial huair Te juhj and Te spren audiens auait .Tey diskus Te terms of Te trial and seker kaulz his uitneses .Te furst amuna bringz too of her deadeye Cahrjez and testifies too Te travestee Tat killed Tem .Te sekond uitnes is blended .Se not onlee says Tat humans Sould not bee trusted but Tat Tey mahyt even konsahyder bonding uiT Te fyoozed as Te rahytful airs of Te land .houever Se uould raTer no bonds hapen in aurder too ensure Tat no spren uill bee killed .Te Tird uitnes is notum .he uhpeers in uniform and adolin realahzs Tat notum has been told Tat if he uill testifee against adolin Tey uill end his eXahyl .notum begins too speak loohking at Te peyper he braut but Ten stohpz and says honor is not ded so long as he livez in Te hearts of men .Te kroud erupts and notum kontinues too bellau Tis freez as he is dragged frohm Te room .adolin goes too speak too kelek and asks huat hapened .kelek tells him Tat Te steykez of Tis trial ahr muC mohr Tan he Taut bekauz suhm spren ahr konsidering joahyning Te hahyspren and Te fyoozed and adolin kould tip Te skales in Te ohposahyt direkSuhn

.after Te trial #[Sallan](characters/shallan) klimbs a tree and faulz too Te ground feyking a severe injuree .pattern plays along and Tey konvins Te honorspren too teyk her too huair tair stormlahyt is kept .Tey do so and Se steals a sfere replasing it uiT a dun uuhn and pretending Tat Se had draahyned in too heal herself .Te honorspren ahr apuhuled at hau Se kould konsume so muC stormlahyt and Tey Suffle her frohm Te room

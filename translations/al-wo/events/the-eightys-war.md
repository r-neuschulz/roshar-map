# Te eahyteez uar

.Te eahyteez uar begahn betueen tukar and emul

## Details

.Te eahyteez uar uas a militaree konflikt on roSar betueen Te bordering naSuhns of emul and tukar during Te tahym of Te aleTi uar of rekohning .Te konflikt arohz over Te sitee of sesemaleX dar huiC is situeyted on Te border betueen Te kingdoms .emul klaahymed Te sitee as tair kapital but Te tukari uanted it fohr Temselvez

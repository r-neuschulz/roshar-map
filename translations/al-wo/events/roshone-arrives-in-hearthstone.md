# roSohn arrivez in hearTstohn

.eXahyled frohm kholinar bahy dalinar roSohn arrived as sitylord of hearTstohn

## Details

.in kholinar roSohn yoozed his konnekSuhns too hav a pair of elderlee darkeyes moaSz grandparents arrested on fals Cahrjez .Te pair dahyed in prison and huen dalinar found out he had Te man eXahyled .roSohn uas plast as Te sitylord of hearTstohn huair dalinar uhsumed he kould kauz no harm .upon his arrival roSohn Saued no respekt fohr Te inhabitants and apeaired partikularlee distasteful of lirin hoom he bleymed fohr Te deaT of Te previous sitylord

# dalinar and sadeas joint uhsaults

.after sadeas kleaired dalinarz neym Te too agreed too attempt joint plateau uhsaults .Te furst uas suhksesful and Tey desahyded too kontinue

## Details

.Te tenzhuhn betueen dalinar and sadeas eeezed after Te latterz investigaSuhn into king elhokarz saddl strap revealed Tat dalinar uas lahyklee freymed .Te too diskused dalinarz proposal of a joint plateau uhsault and agreed too look fohr Te neXt ohpertunitee too tree Te stratijee out .Te furst attempt uas a suhkses uiT Te armies uinning Te gemheart and dalinar seyving sadeasez lahyf .Te too agreed too kontinue implementing Te stratijee in Te ueeks too kuhm

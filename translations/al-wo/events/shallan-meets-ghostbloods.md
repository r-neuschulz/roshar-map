# Sallan meets gohstbloods

.Sallan entered Te uarkamps and met uiT Te gohstbloods fohr Te furst tahym

## Details

.on her furst nahyt in Te uarkamps Sallan found tynz spanreed flaSing .Se responded too tynz mesenjer suhksesfullee imiteyting Te uoman and learned Tat Te gohstbloods desired too meet uiTin Te hour .unuhbuhl too delaee Te meeting Sallan eXplaahyned Tat Se  tyn  uas uounded and Tat her aprentis uould uhpeer fohr Te meeting insted .Sallan praktist her lahytueeyving as Se uaahyted fohr Te meeting too bee koordineyted inventing a fals identitee too yooz as tynz suhpohzed aprentis  a fals identitee Se uould soon kuhm too neym veil .uiT everyTing aurgahnahyzed and her identitee kovered bahy an illuzhuhn Sallan snuk out of her room and into Te nahyt .Sallan evenCuallee lokahyted Te building Se had been direkted too in sebarialz kamp .Te guard at Te entruhns allaued her too enter and Ten led her doun into a basement and Trou a trapdoor .in Te steytlee room belau Sallan met mraahz and an audiens of silent onloohkerz inkluding masked iyatil .Toh disapointed not too see tyn herself mraahz desahyded too spair veilz lahyf intrteeged bahy her boldnes .he asked Sallan hoom he kaulz litl knahyf too hav tyn infiltreyt brahytlord amaramz palas in searC of hidden sekrets .Sallan Ten left Te hideout and returned hohm yoozing a lahytueeyving along Te uaee too elude a pursuing iyatil

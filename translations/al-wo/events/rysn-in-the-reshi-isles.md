# rysn in Te reSi iahylez

.rysn and vstim treyded uiT Te reSi .rysn beseym paralyzed but uas gifted a larkin

## Details

.rysn and vstim arrived in Te reSi iahylez seeking too treyd uiT Te inhabitants of a tai na greatSell .vstim pretended too bee deaTlee ill and rekuired rysn too meyk Te treyd in his plas as a test .Tinking tair livelihood must bee on Te lahyn rysn took despareyt measures too meyk Te treyd fauling frohm Te greatSell in an attempt too prove her braveree .rysn uas left paralyzed frohm Te uaist doun after Te insident .Te island gifted her uiT a living larkin
